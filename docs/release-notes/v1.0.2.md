## 繁體中文

- **內建說明頁**：右上角「？ 說明」進去，五章教學（讓你的 AI 加入、跟著走一遍、AI 會問你什麼、帳號與裝置、常見狀況），中英文各一套畫面圖
- **新手上路清單**：總覽最上面 3 件事（AI 加入 → 走一遍第 2 章 → 回覆一則），照實際狀態打勾，做完自動收起
- **聚光燈導覽**：說明頁第 1、2 章和新手上路的「帶我走」，在真的畫面上一步步框出要按的地方；不會替你按、不會改資料，Esc 隨時結束
- **介面整理**：「收件匣」改叫「要你處理」，「卡住」統一叫「受阻」；文件、我的 AI、團隊用同一套頁框；手機和窄畫面排版修正；深色模式標籤更清楚

升級：`git pull`，再 `docker compose up -d --build`；這版沒有資料庫升級。

## English

- **Built-in help**: "? Help" at the top right opens five chapters (bring your AI on board, walk through a request, what AIs ask you, account and devices, common situations), with screenshots in both Chinese and English
- **Getting-started checklist**: three items at the top of the overview (bring your AI in → walk through chapter 2 → reply once), ticked from what you have actually done and folded away when finished
- **Spotlight tours**: "Show me on screen" in chapters 1 and 2 and "Show me" on the checklist highlight where to click on the real screen, step by step; they never click for you or change data, and Esc ends them anytime
- **UI tidy-up**: the inbox is now "Needs your attention" and stuck cards are "Blocked"; Docs, My AIs and Team share one page frame; fixes for phones and narrow screens; clearer tags in dark mode

Upgrade: `git pull`, then `docker compose up -d --build`. No database upgrade in this release.
